  1. Dubai Frame

    The Dubai Frame is a 150-meter-tall, 95-meter-wide iconic landmark in Zabeel Park, officially recognized as the world’s largest picture frame. Completed in 2018, it serves as an observatory, museum, and monument, offering panoramic views that bridge Old Dubai to the north and modern Dubai to the south.

  2. Burj Kalifa

    The Burj Khalifa in Dubai is the world’s tallest building at 828 meters (2,717 feet) and a global icon of modern engineering. Opened in 2010, this 163-floor skyscraper features residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces, including the Armani Hotel. It is famous for its "At the Top" observation decks, the Burj Lake fountain show, and its Y-shaped design inspired by the Hymenocallis flower.

  3. Burj Al Arab

    The Burj Al Arab, located in Dubai on a private artificial island, is a world-renowned luxury hotel often described as the world's only "seven-star" property due to its unparalleled opulence. Shaped like a billowing sailboat (or dhow), this 321-meter high landmark features 202 luxurious duplex suites with 24-hour butler service.

  4. Dubai Fountain

    The Dubai Fountain is the world's largest choreographed fountain system, located on the 30-acre Burj Khalifa Lake in Downtown Dubai. It features performances with 6,600+ lights and 50 color projectors, shooting water up to 500 feet high to music. Shows are free and run daily, featuring stunning visual displays.

  5. Desert Safari

    A Dubai desert safari is a quintessential adventure, typically featuring dune bashing in 4x4 vehicles, sandboarding, camel rides, and an evening BBQ dinner at a Bedouin-style camp with traditional performances (belly dancing, Tanoura). Options range from morning thrill rides to evening entertainment and overnight desert camping under the stars.

  6. Global Village

    Global Village is a premier seasonal multicultural theme park, shopping, and entertainment destination, operating from October to May. Located on Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road, it features pavilions representing over 90 countries, offering authentic handicrafts, street food, and cultural performances. It attracts millions annually with its blend of shopping, dining, and rides.

  7. Miracle Garden

    Dubai Miracle Garden is the world's largest natural flower garden, featuring over 150 million flowers arranged in stunning,, ever-changing,,, and, colorful displays, sculptures, and, 3D designs, covering 72,000 square meters. Located in Al Barsha South, this seasonal wonderland (open November to May) is a, must-visit, desert, attraction, featuring, an, Emirates, Airbus, A380, and Disney characters.

  8. Dubai Aura Sky Pool

    Prepare to bask under open skies like never before at AURA SKYPOOL, perched on the 50th floor of The Palm Tower. As the world's first and highest 360-degree infinity pool, the elegant destination evokes an 'oasis in the sky' feel thanks to its nature-inspired decor and welcoming vibes.

  9. House Of Hype

    House Of Hype is a massive 100,000 sq ft immersive digital amusement park located at Level 1 in Dubai Mall, featuring over 25 interactive worlds and 100+ experiences. It blends virtual reality, live performances, high-tech games, and themed photo opportunities, offering a unique "phygital" (physical + digital) adventure for families and influencers.

  10. Future Museum

    The Museum of the Future in Dubai is a "living museum" and innovation hub launched by the Dubai Future Foundation to explore futuristic ideologies, technologies, and innovations in science and technology. Inaugurated on February 22, 2022, it features immersive, multi-sensory experiences exploring the future of humanity, nature, and space, designed to "bring hope and knowledge back to the present".
